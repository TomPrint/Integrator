4. Scope and technical requirements

The portal downloads the specified CSV file, allows columns to be selected and transformed, and makes the result available at an individual link. Updates take place at the selected frequency, subject to source and infrastructure availability. Use requires internet access, an up-to-date browser supporting cookies and JavaScript, and a directly accessible HTTP or HTTPS address for the CSV file.

The source and output files may each be at most 20 MiB; the source file may contain up to 200 columns and 200,000 data rows. Files exceeding these limits will not be accepted or published. For a source of at least 5 MiB, the minimum interval between updates is 24 hours. If a source reaches this size after faster updates have been configured, the portal automatically extends the interval to 24 hours and informs the customer in the dashboard. A file within the limits may still fail to be processed, for example due to load, source response time or hosting limitations. Before concluding a contract, the parties may check the required files and agree on whether they can be handled; if they cannot, the provider may decline the order.
