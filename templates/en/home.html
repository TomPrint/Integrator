How it works

Adapt data to your workflow.

Connect a source

Enter an integration name and the full, direct HTTP or HTTPS address of the wholesaler's CSV file. Mid-Grator downloads the file, detects its delimiter and reads its columns. Then select the columns you need and adapt their names to your system's requirements.

Transform data

Change the contents of selected columns, for example replacing a numeric stock level with text or “available” with a number. Set your own conditions, such as a stock level of 10 or more as “high quantity”. You can also increase or decrease prices in a selected column by a chosen percentage, for example by 10%.

Publish and update

Download the result using your individual link or give the link to your shop's integrator. The file is updated at the frequency you choose.
