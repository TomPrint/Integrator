Privacy policy

We explain how we process personal data and use cookies on the Mid-Grator portal.

2. Data we process

We process data provided in correspondence and in managing an account, including contact details, company details, billing details, username and a hashed password. We also record the date, version hash and a copy of the source of the terms template accepted by the user, and associate the acceptance with the integration added. The portal also stores integration settings, source CSV file addresses and generated files. The infrastructure may record technical data such as IP address, request time, URL and browser information. If customer files contain personal data, the parties establish their respective roles before processing. Where a data processing agreement is required, the parties enter into it before processing such data.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We process data to respond to enquiries, enter into and perform contracts and manage accounts (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, where applicable, or (f) — our legitimate interest in customer service and business contacts). We also process billing data to comply with legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)). Security, diagnostics and the establishment of or defence against claims are based on our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)). Optional analytics is based on consent (Article 6(1)(a)).

4. Cookies and browser storage

Essential cookies are used, among other things, to maintain sessions and protect against request forgery. We store your analytics choice, its version and date in local storage for up to 12 months. You can accept or reject optional analytics or choose it in the settings; you can change your decision later using “Cookie settings” in the footer. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

5. Google Analytics

If we configure Google Analytics 4, its script will be loaded only after you consent to analytics. It may collect information about pages visited, events, devices and browsers, as well as cookie identifiers such as “_ga”. When consent is withdrawn, we disable measurement, communicate denial and delete analytics cookies accessible to the portal. Advertising consent signals remain disabled. The retention period of data in Google depends on the service configuration; Google Analytics cookies may be stored for up to 2 years.

6. Recipients and transfers

Data may be received by hosting, email, accounting and technical service providers, as well as other entities entitled by law. If analytics is enabled and consent is given, Google may receive data. Use of Google services may involve transfers outside the European Economic Area; appropriate mechanisms required by the GDPR are used, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

7. Retention period

We delete active account and integration data, including output files and configuration, within 30 days after the contract ends, except for data that must or may be retained longer under the law, including for accounting and the establishment of or defence against claims. Data in backups is deleted according to the backup retention cycle; details are available by email. Accounting data is retained for the period required by law. Correspondence and logs are retained for as long as needed to handle the matter, ensure security or protect claims.

8. Your rights

Under the conditions set out in the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of and transfer your data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time. You can also lodge a complaint with the President of the Polish Personal Data Protection Office. To exercise your rights, contact us by email.

9. Voluntary provision and automated decisions

Providing data is voluntary, but some data is needed to contact us, conclude a contract, handle billing and use an account. We do not make decisions about individuals based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect them.
